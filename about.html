Our Story

IP Printers has been designing and manufacturing packaging for businesses in Faisalabad and across Pakistan for more than 20 years. In that time, our approach hasn't changed: every box and bag we produce is made in-house, from raw board to finished product, using materials we refuse to compromise on.

Whether it's a small custom order or an ongoing supply line, our production runs on the same standard we started with two decades ago — reliable quality, delivered on time.
